Post Grad Moving Checklist

A move has hundreds of small tasks, and the ones you forget are usually the ones that cost money. This post grad moving checklist is organized by timeline so you can tackle a little at a time. Print it, screenshot it, or keep this page open on your phone and work down the list.

Short on time? If your move is less than three weeks away, start with the "Two weeks to go" section and read the earlier sections for anything you can still do.

Eight weeks to go

  • Confirm your start date, salary, and first paycheck date with your employer.
  • Set a moving budget that includes a deposit, travel costs, and a cash cushion.
  • Research neighborhoods and decide on a target rent range.
  • Ask about relocation assistance or a signing bonus, and get any offer in writing.
  • Start sorting belongings into keep, sell or donate, and toss piles.

Six weeks to go

  • Begin your apartment search and book tours, in person or by video.
  • Collect documents landlords ask for, such as an offer letter, pay stubs, ID, and references.
  • Get quotes from moving companies, truck rentals, or container services.
  • Check your current lease for the notice period you must give before leaving.
  • Reserve a truck or movers once you know your move date.

Four weeks to go

  • Sign your new lease and pay the deposit using a traceable method.
  • Give written notice to your current landlord if you rent.
  • Schedule utility start dates for electricity, gas, water, and internet at the new address.
  • Book time off or confirm the days you need for the move.
  • Order boxes, tape, and packing paper, or collect free boxes from local stores.

Two weeks to go

  • Submit a change of address with the postal service.
  • Update your address with your bank, credit card companies, student loan servicer, and insurance providers.
  • Transfer or refill prescriptions and find a doctor, dentist, and pharmacy near the new home.
  • Request medical and dental records if you plan to switch providers.
  • Buy renter's insurance and set the start date to your move-in day.
  • Pack items you rarely use, and label every box by room and contents.

The week of the move

  • Confirm times with movers or your truck rental company.
  • Defrost the freezer and use up perishable food.
  • Pack an essentials bag with a phone charger, medications, toiletries, a change of clothes, important papers, and snacks.
  • Take photos of your old apartment before you leave to document its condition.
  • Clean the old place and return keys as your lease requires.
  • Cancel or transfer services at the old address, such as internet and electricity.

Move-in day

  • Walk through the new apartment before you unload and photograph any damage.
  • Test the smoke detectors, locks, faucets, and outlets, and report problems in writing.
  • Set up your bed and kitchen first so the first night is comfortable.
  • Find the circuit breaker and the water shutoff valve.
  • Keep copies of your lease and the move-in condition report in a safe place.

Your first two weeks

  • Update your driver's license and vehicle registration according to your new state's deadline.
  • Update your voter registration at the new address.
  • Ask your employer to update your address and tax withholding information if needed.
  • Learn your commute, trash pickup days, and the nearest grocery store.
  • Introduce yourself to a neighbor and save the building manager's contact information.
  • Look for a gym, library, club, or volunteer group so you meet people outside of work.

Keep going

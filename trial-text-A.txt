Your first big move, made manageable

Graduation day comes with a lot of excitement and one very large question: what happens next? If your answer involves a new city, a new job, and a new address, you are in the right place. The Landing Zone is a friendly guide for recent graduates who are packing up and starting over somewhere unfamiliar.

We keep things simple. Every guide here is written in plain language, built around a realistic budget, and designed to be read on your phone while you are standing in a hardware store or sitting in a parking lot outside an apartment tour.

Who we are

The Landing Zone was dreamed up by Maya Reyes, who left her college town in the Midwest with two suitcases, a used futon, and a job offer in a city where she did not know a single person. Her first month included a missed utility deposit, a sublet that fell through, and a lot of takeout eaten on the floor. She started writing down what she wished someone had told her. Those notes became this site.

Maya now works with housing editor Theo Lindqvist, who has toured more than two hundred apartments and can tell you within thirty seconds whether a bathroom fan actually works.

What you can expect from us

- Honest numbers. We talk about deposits, fees, and the cost of a first month so you can plan around them.
- Short steps. Big tasks are broken into small ones you can finish in an afternoon.
- No judgment. Whether you are moving two hours away or across the country, with a roommate or on your own, the advice here is for you.

Not sure where to begin? If you have a job offer or a target city but no plan yet, read the moving guide first. If your move date is already close, jump straight to the checklist.

Your first month in a new city

The first few weeks of settling into a new city are mostly about small wins: getting your mail forwarded, learning your commute, and finding a grocery store you like. Our Moving Guide walks you through a timeline and budget so each week has a clear goal. Take it one task at a time and the new place will start to feel like home.
